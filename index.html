The Assignment Problem

One driver per rider. Lowest total distance.

What the problem is

There are n free drivers and n waiting riders. Each driver is a different distance from each rider. Give every rider exactly one driver, and every driver exactly one rider, to minimise the total distance driven to pickups.

Why it matters

Apps like Uber, Ola and Rapido solve this thousands of times per minute. Shorter pickups mean faster rides for riders, less fuel for drivers and fewer idle cars. The same problem assigns delivery partners to orders and workers to tasks.

What the program computes

From your driver and rider coordinates it builds a distance table, then finds the pairing and total distance using brute force, greedy, dynamic programming and backtracking, and compares them.
